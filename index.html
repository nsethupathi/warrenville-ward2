Where I Stand

The Issues That Brought Me In

Two problems residents raise again and again.

Remove the Flock Cameras

Warrenville residents have repeatedly asked the council to remove the Flock license-plate cameras installed around our neighborhoods. That request has gone unanswered. I'll push to take them down and put decisions like this back in residents' hands, not on autopilot.

Fix Our Water Hardness

Warrenville's water tests at 24–26 grains per gallon of hardness — more than double the 10.5-grain threshold that defines "very hard" water. That means more scale buildup and shorter-lived appliances. I'll push the city to explore central softening so residents aren't left to solve it individually.
